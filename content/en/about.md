I'm **Patrizia Märki**. I discovered Pilates in 2011 and found in it the union of my two passions: movement and teaching.

I earned my diploma as a gymnastics and fitness instructor in 1989 and studied dance in the United States. For years I taught at fitness studios in Zurich before dedicating myself fully to Pilates.

At the 2015 Classical Pilates Convention in Pisa I met **Jay Grimes**, a direct student of Joseph Pilates, and **Gloria Gasperi**. They deeply inspired me with their view of movement as a way of healing and teaching, and the importance of developing one's own intuition and personal expression.

My teaching is built on **passion, quality, integrity, the joy of sharing, and helping people feel good.**

I teach in Spanish, English, Italian, German and French.

## Certifications

- **Master Program** — Pilates E-Motion Teachers Educational School, Sarzana, Italy (2019–2020)
- **Comprehensive Certified Teacher** — The Pilates Standard, Uster, Switzerland (2012–2013)
- **Personal Trainer** — SAFS Swiss Academy (2000)
- **Diploma in Gymnastics and Fitness Instruction** (1988–1989)

## Continuing education

- Seminars with PMA-recognized international Master Teachers (2013–2018)
- Specialized courses in pelvic floor, posture, back health, relaxation techniques and dance teaching
