## Getting started

Everything begins with a **free consultation**, where we talk about your wishes, needs and goals. This is followed by **three introductory sessions** covering the fundamentals of the technique.

Training takes place on the apparatus and on the mat, in a personal atmosphere with flexible scheduling.

## Formats

- **Private sessions** — one-on-one, fully tailored to you
- **Semi-private** — up to 3 people
- **Mat classes** — groups of 3–4 people
- **Online private sessions**
