Hola Patrizia! 👋

This page is just for you. It isn't in the menu and search engines won't show it, but anyone who has the link can open it, so there is nothing secret here.

**You never have to edit your website yourself.** You tell Claude what you want, in your own words and in any language, and Claude does the work.

> **Don't worry:** you can't break anything permanently. Every change is saved, and you can always say *"Undo my last change"*.

---

## Two places to use Claude

| | What it's for |
|---|---|
| 💻 **Claude on your Mac** | **Changing your website**, and asking questions |
| 📱 **Claude on your phone** | **Asking questions** and learning, anywhere |

---

## Getting started (once)

1. **Install Claude on your Mac** from <https://claude.ai/download> and sign in.
2. **Create a free GitHub account** at <https://github.com/signup>. GitHub is where your website is stored.
3. **Accept the invitation** to the **patrizia-pilates** project that arrives in your email.
4. **Open Claude on your Mac**, go to the **Code** section, and ask:

   > *"Please set up my website project 'patrizia-pilates' from GitHub on this Mac. I'm a beginner, so guide me step by step."*

If you get stuck on any step, ask Claude (on your phone or Mac) and send it a screenshot: *"What do I do next?"*

---

## Changing your website

1. Open **Claude on your Mac** → **Code** → your **patrizia-pilates** project.
2. **Say what you want:**

   > *"On the Clases page, a private session costs 900 pesos. Please update Spanish and English."*

3. **Ask to see it:** *"Show me a preview."*
4. **Publish:** *"Looks good, please publish it."*
5. After a few minutes, it's live at **<https://david-williston.github.io/patrizia-pilates/>** 🎉

If Claude asks permission to do something, read it and tap **Allow** if it makes sense. If not, ask *"What does that mean?"*

## Ideas for what to ask

- *"Fill in the prices on the Clases page: …"*
- *"Add my Instagram: @…"*
- *"Add the studio address: …"*
- *"Use the photo 'me.jpg' from my Downloads folder on the Sobre mí page."*
- *"Add a new class called 'Pilates for surfers', in both languages."*
- *"Make the purple a little softer."*
- *"What information is still missing from my website?"*
- *"Undo my last change."*

## Learning with Claude 📱

Use Claude on your phone (or Mac) as your patient teacher. Ask anything, in any language:

> *"I'm a beginner. What is GitHub? Explain it simply, like you'd explain something to a Pilates student."*

> *"¿Qué significa 'publicar' un sitio web?"*

> *"Spiegami cos'è un dominio."*

**Tips:** say you're a beginner, ask "can you explain that differently?" whenever you need to, and send a screenshot when something on your screen is confusing.

---

## Good to know

- **Check before publishing.** Read Claude's summary of what it changed, or ask for a preview.
- **Both languages.** Your site is in Spanish and English. Claude updates both, but remind it if it forgets.
- **Never share passwords** or bank details in a chat.
- **Claude can make mistakes.** If something looks wrong, tell it: *"The Clases page looks strange now, can you fix it?"*
